## What this site does

WordCraftApp helps readers learn about the WordCraft word processor, find official desktop downloads, and work through document editing tasks. We publish beginner guides, compatibility checklists, browser troubleshooting steps and practical tutorials for everyday documents. Our [homepage](/) brings these resources together; the [blog](/blog) lists the full reading collection.

We also host a browser build of the open-source editor. The editor is developed by the upstream WordCraft project, not by this website. Our [download page](/download) links to upstream release assets. The [hosted-build notice](/app/NOTICE.md) identifies the browser version, its source and the changes made to its hosting shell.

## Who maintains WordCraftApp

WordCraftApp is maintained by an independent developer. For questions about this website, broken links, inaccurate instructions or suggestions for new articles, use our [contact page](/contact) or email [support@wordcraftapp.com](mailto:support@wordcraftapp.com).

The site maintainer is responsible for this site's content and navigation. Product development, upstream release decisions and the WordCraft project itself remain with their respective maintainers. Contacting this website does not open an official WordCraft support ticket.

## How our content is prepared

Our articles are original explanations built around concrete document tasks. We consult public upstream documentation, release notes and source code, and link to relevant sources so readers can check feature claims. Where an article describes a proposed workflow or an evaluation checklist, it is guidance for readers to try with their own files, not a claim that every combination of platform, browser and document has been tested.

Examples use sample text and illustrative document data. Screenshots credited to ArtCraft / WordCraft come from upstream project materials and may show a different release or platform. We do not present those images as proof of a test performed by this site. Compatibility depends on document features, installed fonts and the receiving editor; we do not assign an invented compatibility percentage or publish unsupported performance benchmarks.

Articles display their publication or update date. A later release may change controls or behavior. If an instruction no longer matches your installation, send the page URL, the WordCraft version and a short description through [Contact](/contact). Corrections can then be checked against the relevant release and incorporated into the article.
