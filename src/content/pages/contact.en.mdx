## Email the site maintainer

Contact us at **[support@wordcraftapp.com](mailto:support@wordcraftapp.com)**. You can use your own email application; no website account or contact form is required.

We aim to reply within **3–5 business days**.

## What to include

For a correction to an article, include the page URL, the sentence or step that needs attention, and what you expected to see. If a feature has changed, a link to the relevant official release or documentation helps us check the issue.

For a problem with the hosted browser editor, include your browser and operating system, the version shown in the [build notice](/app/NOTICE.md), and whether the problem happens during loading, opening, editing or saving. A short sequence of steps using a blank document is more useful than a large private file. Remove names, addresses and other sensitive information from any example you choose to send. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.

## Website questions and product support

This address is for the independent WordCraftApp website, including its guides, links and hosted editor page. We do not operate the upstream WordCraft project and cannot make commitments on behalf of its developers.

For an upstream software bug, consult the [official issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft/issues) and its reporting guidance. For a website issue or an unclear article, email us directly. If you are unsure where a problem belongs, describe it and include the page you were using.

Read [About WordCraftApp](/about) for our maintenance and content policy, or return to the [guides on the homepage](/#guides).
